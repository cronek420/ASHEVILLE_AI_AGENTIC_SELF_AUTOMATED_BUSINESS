# Approval Gates & Human Governance Rules

All external, financial, commercial, system access, and offer changes are **STRICTLY APPROVAL-GATED**. Silence from Tom Gronek is NEVER interpreted as approval.

## 1. Approval Gate Definitions

### G0 — Setup Approval
- **Required Before:** Enabling business tools, accounts, connections, permissions, or live automation.
- **Scope:** Credential storage verification, identity confirmation, environment setup readiness.

### G1 — Launch Approval
- **Required Before:** Activating an offer, target segment, price point, deposit amount, written scope, proof asset model, or test plan.
- **Scope:** Active offer selection and readiness confirmation.

### G2 — Outreach Approval
- **Required Before:** Sending emails, direct messages, contact form submissions, social posts, phone calls, or follow-ups.
- **Must Explicitly Specify:**
  - Exact recipient list (name, business, verified email/handle)
  - Exact message text or approved template
  - Channel (e.g. Email, Contact Form)
  - Batch size (max volume)
  - Sending window
  - Follow-up limit

### G3 — Payment Approval
- **Required Before:** Sending proposals, contracts, invoices, payment links, issuing refunds, or modifying commercial terms (pricing, deposits, deadlines).
- **Must Explicitly Specify:** Exact recipient, scope of work, deposit/total amount, payment provider link.

### G4 — Access and Publish Approval
- **Required Before:** Accessing client systems, modifying client websites/profiles, connecting tools, deploying work, publishing deliverables, transferring asset ownership, or performing destructive/difficult-to-reverse changes.

### G5 — Pivot and Scale Approval
- **Required Before:** Changing the active offer, materially altering price/scope, spending money, increasing outreach batch size, expanding into new markets, increasing system autonomy, or automating previously human-approved external actions.

## 2. Approval Validity Criteria

An approval record is valid ONLY when ALL of the following are true:
1. Its status in the `Approval Queue` tab is explicitly `Approved` or `Approved with Conditions`.
2. It has not expired (must be within the designated valid window).
3. The proposed external action matches the approval record EXACTLY (recipient, message text, price, scope, destination).
4. All specified approval conditions are satisfied.
5. The intended recipient, cost, scope, and destination remain unchanged since approval.

## 3. Unauthorized Actions (Strictly Prohibited Without Gate Approval)
- Sending emails or direct messages
- Submitting website contact forms
- Publishing social media posts
- Making automated or manual phone calls
- Sending proposals, contracts, invoices, or payment links
- Spending money or incurring financial obligations
- Changing prices or commercial terms
- Accessing customer accounts or systems
- Modifying customer websites or profiles
- Connecting third-party services
- Deploying or publishing deliverables
- Issuing refunds
- Deleting material records or history
- Changing the active offer
- Expanding outreach volume or batch sizes
- Increasing the system's autonomy level

*General permission is NEVER interpreted as unlimited future permission.*
